## The Question
A root verb can be small enough to overlook and large enough to reorganize an entire field of interpretation. This study begins with repeated verbal forms and asks what happens when we treat them as active structures rather than dictionary entries.

> Meaning does not live in the glossary alone. It lives in the relationship between word, speaker, context, and world.

## Separation Before Synthesis
The research method begins by separating recurring forms from the larger structures in which they appear. Once those pieces can be observed clearly, they can be returned to the whole without pretending the whole was ever merely the sum of its parts.
